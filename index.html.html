SUPPORT & GUIDANCE

Help centre

Quick explanations for common onboarding tasks.

What does “verification review” mean?

The onboarding team or verification partner is checking the submitted business and identity details. If extra information is needed, the client should see a specific request.

When should I request more documents?

Request only the evidence required to complete the current check. Include a clear reason and a next step so the client knows what to upload.

How are approval decisions handled?

An authorised reviewer can approve a request, reject it with a reason, or return it for more information. Keep the decision and rationale in the audit trail.
